# Alpha 0x Building: academy

## Basic Info
- **ID**: academy
- **Name**: Академия
- **Era**: Classical Era (Era 3) — unlocks after `mathematics` technology
- **Cost**: 70 stone + 30 gold
- **Workers**: 4 per shift
- **Daily Output**: +2.2 knowledge per day
- **Special**: Center of learning — preserves and advances civilization knowledge
- **Requirement**: `mathematics` technology must be researched
- **Evolves Into**: university, research institute, digital knowledge network

## Alpha 0x Logic Enhancements

### 1. Knowledge Generation System
- **Knowledge Output**: 2.2 knowledge per day — accelerates technology research by ~25% in classical era
- **Research Acceleration**: Reduces technology research cost by 10% when academy is active
- **Great Person Generation**: 1 great person point per 50 days of accumulated knowledge
- **Library Integration**: Connected libraries (via aura) gain +15% knowledge output bonus
- **Inter-institution Transfer**: Knowledge can be "shared" between adjacent academies (up to 3 nearby) — +0.1 knowledge/day per connection

### 2. Evolution Path (Alpha 0x Upgrade System)
The academy can evolve into **3 different directions** based on knowledge focus:

#### Evolution A: University (research expansion)
- **New Name**: Университет
- **Cost**: 90 stone + 50 gold (increased from academy cost)
- **Workers**: 5 (increased from 4)
- **Daily Output**: +4.0 knowledge per day (increased from 2.2)
- **Special**: Institution of higher learning — structured research programs, student housing, doctoral candidates
- **Visual**: Grand building with columns, lecture halls, library stacks, student quadrangle, academic regalia
- **Unlock**: Available after mathematics tech (era 3) — primary knowledge building

#### Evolution B: Research Institute (specialized knowledge)
- **New Name**: Научно-исследовательский институт
- **Cost**: 50 stone + 40 gold + 20 steel (modern materials)
- **Workers**: 3 (reduced from 5 — more focused research)
- **Daily Output**: +2.8 knowledge + +0.5 specialized knowledge per day
- **Special**: Deep research — focuses on one technology branch (science, engineering, medicine), 1.5× research speed in chosen field
- **Visual**: Laboratory modules, equipment rooms, data analysis chambers, professor offices, specialized equipment
- **Unlock**: Available after philosophy + mathematics (era 4)

#### Evolution C: Digital Knowledge Network (future era)
- **New Name**: Цифровой знания сеть
- **Cost**: 200 stone + 150 gold + 100 steel + 50 crystals (future tech)
- **Workers**: 2 (automated AI management)
- **Daily Output**: +10.0 knowledge per day (4.5× original, AI-optimized research)
- **Special**: Global knowledge network — all civilization knowledge connected, AI research suggestions, instant knowledge sharing across cities
- **Visual**: Floating data spheres, holographic knowledge maps, server clusters, ambient glowing interfaces, sleek futuristic design
- **Unlock**: Available after AI tech (era 9-10)

### 3. Alpha 0x Interactions

#### With Other Buildings
- **Academy + University (evolved)**: Direct lineage — university receives +20% knowledge bonus from academy heritage
- **Academy + University (multiple)**: Synergistic — 2+ academies/universities create knowledge network ×1.5 total bonus
- **Academy + Story Fire (evolved)**: Cultural knowledge link — combined output +15% vs individual
- **Academy + Hospital**: Medical research — knowledge accelerated for medicine technologies +20%
- **Academy + Barracks**: Military theory — knowledge gain from warfare studies +0.1 knowledge/day

#### With Technologies & Eras
- **Mathematics tech prerequisite**: Academy cannot be built without `mathematics` — ensures proper tech tree progression
- **Era scaling**: Knowledge output scales (2.2 → 4.0 → 2.8+specialized → 10.0 AI-optimized)
- **Great person generation**: Each academy contributes to great person pool — faster with more academies

#### With Events
- **Insight event**: 33% chance per day — knowledge +15, happiness +2 (enhanced if great person present)
- **Eureka moment**: Random event — +50% knowledge for 5 days, technology research accelerated
- **Brain drain**: 25% of knowledgeable residents leave for other civilizations — temporary -0.5 knowledge/day
- **Endowment**: Wealthy patron donates — +20 knowledge instantly, +1 happiness permanently

### 4. Gameplay Balance & Alpha 0x Tuning

#### Early Game Impact (Days 200-300, after mathematics tech)
- **Knowledge generation**: 2.2/day — significant research acceleration
- **Tech research speed**: Academy reduces tech research time by ~25%
- **Great person pool**: Contributes to great person generation — strategic for late-game super-buildings

#### Mid-Game Transition (Days 300-500)
- **Evolution decision**: University for broad research, research institute for specialized fields, digital network for endgame knowledge dominance
- **Knowledge race**: Players with academies advance faster in tech tree — major strategic advantage
- **Great person competition**: Multiple academies competing for great person visits and points

#### Late Game (Days 500+)
- **University relevance**: 4.0 knowledge/day — useful through industrial for mid-tier techs and wonders
- **Research institute**: Specialized knowledge — valuable for specific technology paths (medicine, engineering)
- **Digital knowledge network**: 10.0 knowledge/day — endgame essential for space research, AI development, late-game victories
- **Obsolescence**: Original 2.2 knowledge/day becomes supplemental; evolved versions critical for late-game knowledge generation

### 6. Alpha 0x AI Integration

#### Student/Researcher AI Priorities
1. **Knowledge acquisition**: Primary task — gain knowledge from academy within radius
2. **Research projects**: Assigned projects based on civilization's current tech priorities
3. **Great person pursuit**: AI students compete for great person visits/mentorship
4. **Rest**: Return to dorms or homes at day's end if energy < 30%

#### Knowledge AI
- **Research priority evaluation**: AI assesses civilization's current tech needs, directs academy knowledge accordingly
- **Great person matching**: Matches great person type to academy research focus for maximum benefit
- **Inter-academy coordination**: If multiple academies, optimizes knowledge distribution for maximum network effect
- **Future research prediction**: Predicts likely future tech paths, prepares knowledge generation proactively

#### RNG Seed Integration
- Academy seed determines **cultural-research orientation** — affects which great people are attracted, what research paths are favored
- Different seed values produce different "knowledge specializations" (scientific, philosophical, artistic, military)
- Inter-academy network patterns create different knowledge distribution across civilization
- Great person visit patterns create unique research breakthroughs per playthrough

### 8. Alpha 0x Modding Support

#### Extendable Properties
Modders can add:
- **New evolution paths** via `evolve` property
- **Knowledge scaling** via `knowledge_output` property (per era formula)
- **Research focus** via `research_branch` property (science, engineering, medicine, military)
- **Great person boost** via `great_person_chance` property

#### Example Mod Addition
```javascript
// Modder-added academy variant
academy_philosophy: {
  name: 'Философская академия',
  cost: { stone: 60, gold: 25 },
  workers: 4,
  out: { knowledge: 3.0 },
  special: 'philosophy',
  req: 'mathematics',
  desc: 'Knowledge +3.0, +0.1 happiness, great person chance +50% for philosopher type'
}
```

#### Research Branch System
```javascript
// Modder-created research specialization
research_branch: {
  name: 'Инженерные исследования',
  effect: 'tech_research_speed',
  value: 1.3,  // 30% faster engineering tech research
  branch: 'engineering'
}
```

### Summary
The Alpha 0x **academy** civilization's intellectual engine, evolving from modest learning center to digital knowledge network spanning entire civilizations. The evolution path reflects humanity's knowledge journey: basic education → university expansion → specialized research → post-scarcity knowledge networks. Each variant offers distinct strategic flavor: the research expansion university, the focused research institute, or the endgame knowledge network dominance — allowing players to shape their civilization's intellectual trajectory and technological supremacy.
