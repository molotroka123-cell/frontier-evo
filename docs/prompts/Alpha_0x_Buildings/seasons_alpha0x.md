# Alpha 0x Mechanic: Dynamic Seasons System

## Core Enhancement
Deepens the existing 4-season cycle (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) from cosmetic to **critical gameplay system** that fundamentally alters strategy each 25 days.

### Seasonal Mechanics

#### Spring (Day 1-25 of each cycle)
- **Agricultural Boost**: +20% food from farms, pastures, foragers
- **Construction Bonus**: +15% building speed — ideal for expansion
- **Wildlife Activity**: Hunting lodges +30% yield — animals mating/migrating
- **Event Pool**: 3× more events triggered (berry year, first frost warnings)
- **Happiness**: +1 global happiness — "renewal feeling"

#### Summer (Day 26-50)
- **Heat Stress**: -10% worker efficiency if no shade/shields
- **Water Scarcity**: Wells and ponds lose 20% capacity — prioritize water buildings
- **Crop Growth**: Maximum farm output but increased consumption
- **Disease Risk**: +15% chance of minor illness without hospitals
- **Event Pool**: Drought events, heatwaves, thunderstorms

#### Autumn (Day 51-75)
- **Harvest Season**: +30% food from farms — critical for winter stockpiling
- **Animal Preparation**: Pastures animals gain weight — +15% winter yield bonus
- **Trade Peak**: Markets see 50% more caravan activity — optimal for resource export
- **Resource Decay**: Unstored resources lose 10% per week — motivate granary construction
- **Event Pool**: Rich veins, migration events, wolf pack coordination

#### Winter (Day 76-100, or permanent in cold biomes)
- **Farm Inactivity**: Farms produce 0 food — makes pastures essential
- **Housing Priority**: Residents near heated buildings (taverns, temples) get +20% happiness
- **Wood Consumption**: Lumber mills, households consume 2× more wood for heating
- **Disease Penalty**: -30% health without clinics/hospitals
- **Event Pool**: Blizzards, frozen rivers, stranded caravans

### Building Synergies (Alpha 0x Integration)

#### Season-Responsive Buildings
- **Pasture**: Unique building that **works during winter** while farms stop — key survival strategy
- **Granary**: Stores excess spring/summer harvest — provides emergency food buffer (-50% winter penalty)
- **Heater Structures**: Taverns, temples, libraries provide "warmth radius" — residents within gain comfort bonuses
- **Windmills**: Summer bonus +20%, winter penalty -30% — strategic seasonal planning needed

#### Cross-Season Strategy
- **Food Management**: Player must stockpile 200+ food before winter via farm/pasture/hunting balance
- **Resource Rotation**: Different buildings optimal in different seasons — rotate workforce assignments
- **Technology Timing**: Research certain techs in specific seasons (e.g., insulation in autumn)

### Enemy Wave Integration
- **Winter Raids**: Enemy armies attack more frequently in winter (diffending harder)
- **Stockpile Targets**: Armies target resource-rich areas — granaries and markets primary objectives
- **Frozen Terrain**: Movement penalties — armies move 30% slower but defenders gain +15% defense

### Balance & Alpha 0x Tuning
- **Cycle Length**: 25 days × 10 epochs = 250-day seasons — manageable complexity
- **Predictability**: Players can see season coming via calendar — preparation becomes strategic element
- **Difficulty Scaling**: Higher difficulty = longer winters, shorter summers, more severe events

### Modding Support
- **Season Modifiers**: Modders can adjust yield percentages per season
- **Biome Variations**: Desert (no winter), Tundra (permanent winter, harsher penalties)
- **Event Tables**: Custom event pools per season for modded content

### Summary
The Alpha 0x **Dynamic Seasons System** transforms weather from background ambiance into **core strategic layer**. Players must plan 3-4 steps ahead: "Will my food last winter? Should I expand now or prepare? Which buildings get priority this season?" This creates natural pacing variations through the campaign and makes every epoch feel distinct climate-wise.

*From static cycles to dynamic survival — seasons that force tough choices every 25 days.*
